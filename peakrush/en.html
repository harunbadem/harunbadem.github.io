Peak Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Peak Rush (the “app”) is a mobile game developed by Harun Badem (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles.

This version collects no personal data. The app sends nothing to any server, does not ask you to create an account, and does not require an internet connection to play. Your game progress stays on your own device.

Information stored on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app stores the following only in your device’s local storage:

  • Your high score and gameplay statistics (distance travelled, tricks performed, games played, and similar)
  • Badges you have earned and your daily mission progress
  • The outfit colours you have selected
  • Your settings (sound on/off, control sensitivity, language)
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial hints
  • A technical diagnostic log: brief notes about how your recent games ended, used for debugging

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us, to Google, to Apple, or to any other third party. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifier, or advertising identifier. The app contains no analytics tools, no ad networks, and no tracking technology.

Permissions

The app declares only the internet access permission. In this version the app neither sends nor receives data over the internet; the permission is declared for the leaderboard and advertising features planned for a future release. This policy will be updated when those features ship.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. It does not participate in the Google Play Families programme.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you in this version, there is no record that could be subject to an access, correction, or deletion request. You can delete the game data on your device at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s data in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

When advertising and an online leaderboard are added to the app, this policy will be updated, the “last updated” date will change, and the revision will take effect through the app store listing.
